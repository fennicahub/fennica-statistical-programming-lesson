## Possible questions

Some candidates that build on the previous episodes:

- **Publishers:** did the same publishers switch from Swedish to Finnish, or
  did new Finnish-language publishers appear?
- **Places:** did the shift start in the same towns where most books were
  printed, or elsewhere?
- **Authors:** did authors publish in both languages, and did bilingual
  authors move towards Finnish?
- **Publication time:** how does uncertainty in the publication year (for
  example dates such as "185-?") affect the timing of the shift?
