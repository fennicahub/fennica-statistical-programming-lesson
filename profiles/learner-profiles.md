### Aino, PhD student in history

Aino studies the Finnish language movement of the 19th century. She has
collected data in spreadsheets but finds it hard to keep track of how she has
filtered and corrected it. She took an introductory R course, and can load a
CSV file and filter a data frame, but has never worked with library records.
She wants a reproducible way to count and model publications by language and
place that she can describe in the methods chapter of her thesis.

### Mikael, librarian

Mikael works with metadata at a university library and knows MARC records
well. He is often asked by researchers for data extracts, and wants to
understand what they do with the data and help them avoid common pitfalls. He
has used R for simple data extracts and reports, and wants to learn how to
harmonise and validate records systematically.

### Laura, master's student in digital humanities

Laura has taken an introductory statistics course but has not applied it to
real data. She uses R and the tidyverse for course assignments. She wants to practise
the full workflow from raw records to a statistical model, so that she can
start her own project on Finnish literary history.
